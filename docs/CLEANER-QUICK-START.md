# Cleaner quick start

## Your first shift in Neatly

1. Open the link your supervisor shared and sign in.
2. Choose the correct workspace and your role if the shared device asks.
3. Open **Today** or scan the area QR code. You can also navigate to the area from **Areas**.
4. Before starting, check the task name, area, instructions, and estimated minutes.
5. Complete each checklist item. If something is not done, choose the matching option and add a clear note. Report a problem rather than marking it done.
6. Submit the task. It may show **Awaiting verification** until a supervisor reviews it.
7. Report an issue from **Issues** when something needs repair or follow-up. State what and where; do not include people in photos.
8. Use the supply request flow when stock is low. Do not guess consumption or quantities.
9. On a shared device, switch user or sign out when finished. Never share your PIN.

### If you cannot complete a task

Use the swap / can't-do action if available and add a brief reason. Your supervisor can reassign the work. Away mode is available for planned absence. These are coordination tools, not performance scores.

### If a task was completed by mistake

Use **Undo** promptly if it is shown, then complete the task correctly. If a supervisor requests rework, open the linked rework task.

**Printed copy:** use your browser's Print command; this single page is designed as a quick-reference checklist.
