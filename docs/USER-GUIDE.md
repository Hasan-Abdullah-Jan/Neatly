# Neatly user guide

## Start using a space

1. Sign in or create an account.
2. Follow onboarding to choose a personal, shared-home, or organization profile.
3. Add areas and choose the suggested routines that fit. Suggested procedures in a healthcare profile require approval from the organization.
4. Open **Today** to see what is due. Read the task, area, and estimated time before starting.
5. Mark work submitted when complete. Use the task checklist when one is required. A supervisor may verify a submission or request rework.

## Shared spaces

- Invite people from **Invitations** and share the invitation link with the intended person.
- Assign tasks to a person, role, team, or rotation where available.
- Use **Away periods** to pause assignments during travel or illness.
- Use task comments and swap requests to coordinate. Workload is context for balancing tasks, not a ranking.

## Issues and supplies

- Report an issue with a short description and area when known. Photos are optional and may be disabled by the workspace.
- Managers can assign issues and record resolution/verification notes.
- Update supply quantities only when you have information. Low-stock items appear in the shopping list; marking a purchase updates the recorded amount.

## Privacy and support

Use **Settings → Privacy & Security** to export account-associated records or request account deletion. Shared workspace records may be retained and anonymized so other members keep their operational history. See the public [privacy page](../src/features/privacy/pages/PrivacyPage.tsx) for the deployment’s current data notes.

## Try the demo

Open `/demo` to use sample apartment and organization workspaces. Demo changes remain in the browser tab and are never saved to a real account.
